    Welcome to ROXAS AI¶

    ROXAS AI is an open-source, Python-based tool designed to streamline quantitative wood anatomy (QWA) and dendrochronology research. Built as an extensible plugin for the napari multi-dimensional image viewer, it enables researchers to efficiently extract anatomical traits and establish high-resolution time series of cell measurements within dated tree rings.

    Currently optimized for linear conifer anatomies (with linear angiosperm anatomies currently in development), ROXAS AI provides an end-to-end workflow—from automated image segmentation to visual cross-dating—on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

    Why ROXAS AI?¶

    ROXAS AI was developed to modernize and expand upon the foundations of the classical ROXAS implementation. It addresses key limitations of classical workflows to make wood anatomy more accessible, automated, and scalable:

    • Cross-Platform Accessibility

      Runs natively on Windows, macOS, and Linux, bringing quantitative wood anatomy to all operating systems.

    • Open Science & Powerful Viewing

      Built on the open-source napari image viewer, completely eliminating dependencies on proprietary commercial software (such as Image-Pro Plus).

    • Modern AI Segmentation

      Leverages specialized deep-learning neural networks to segment cell lumens and ring boundaries with high accuracy and minimal manual editing.

    • Intuitive End-to-End Workflow

      Combines batch preparation, interactive raster/vector curation, visual cross-dating, and trait extraction into a streamlined, user-friendly interface.

    High-Level Workflow¶

    ROXAS AI guides you through an end-to-end pipeline from raw wood cross-section images to structured anatomical datasets:

    flowchart TD
    A["<b>1. Prepare Images</b><br/>Standardize files & metadata"] --> B["<b>2. AI Segmentation</b><br/>Detect cells & ring boundaries"]
    B --> C["<b>3. Interactive Curation</b><br/>Review & cross-date rings"]
    C --> D["<b>4. Trait Measurement</b><br/>Export CSV tables & plots"]

    Scientific Background & Citation¶

    If you use ROXAS AI in your research, please cite the following publications:

    1. Katzenmaier, M., Peters, R. L., & von Arx, G. (2025). Deep learning for automated tree-ring boundary detection in linear conifer wood anatomy. Frontiers in Plant Science, 16:1516635. doi:10.3389/fpls.2025.1516635

    2. Katzenmaier, M., Peters, R. L., & von Arx, G. (2023). Linear conifer cell segmentation using deep convolutional neural networks. Dendrochronologia, 81:126126. doi:10.1016/j.dendro.2023.126126

    3. von Arx, G., & Carrer, M. (2014). ROXAS - A new tool to build datasets of many thousand computerized wood-anatomical features per sample. Dendrochronologia, 32(3), 290–293. doi:10.1016/j.dendro.2013.12.001
